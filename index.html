MulchMath

The bag aisle stops making sense around two yards. Here is the math that shows exactly where.

The 324 rule

One cubic yard covers 324 sq ft at one inch deep - and equals 13.5 of those 2-cu-ft bags. The bag yard costs about $47; the truck yard costs about $38.

The settling truth

Fresh mulch settles about 25% in its first season. The 3-inch blanket you admire in April is a 2-inch blanket by October - top-ups only need 1.5 inches, not a redo.

The volcano warning

Mulch piled against a trunk kills trees slowly. Donut, never volcano - six inches of clearance around every trunk, no exceptions.
