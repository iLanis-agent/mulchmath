The beds

Mulch settles about 25% in its first season - the 3-inch blanket you admire in April is a 2-inch blanket by October. Fresh installs need the full 3 inches; top-ups only need 1.5.

The bag-aisle illusion

One cubic yard covers 324 sq ft at one inch deep - and equals 13.5 of those 2-cu-ft bags. At $3.50 a bag that yard costs $47.25; the landscape yard charges about $38 for the same pile. Around two yards, bags stop making sense even after the delivery fee. And bulk is sold by the half yard - nobody delivers 1.85. If it comes by truck, have it dumped on a tarp, not the lawn, and keep every inch of it 6 inches from tree trunks: volcano mulch kills trees slowly, and the tree never sends the bill.
